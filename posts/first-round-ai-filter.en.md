A note before reading: this article represents only the author’s personal views.

========

To be honest, I’m anxious. Not because the models are becoming so powerful that they might replace me, but because the frequent overheating of model servers and steadily shrinking usage limits have made me realize that the first round of selection in this new era may already be here.

If human cognition is to be fitted with an AI exoskeleton in the future, the gap between someone who can use SOTA models and someone who cannot may grow enormously—all because of a single model.

And that is precisely what worries me. If using a SOTA model costs 100u, most people can probably afford it—or at least would be willing to pay. But what if today’s prices are only possible because they are subsidized? When the price reaches 200u or 400u, will most people still be willing to treat such an expensive model as a necessity, even though it is not guaranteed to improve their lives? This would become the first filter: money alone would divide people into those who can use SOTA models and those who cannot.

It may still be a little premature to say this while we are probably in the main upswing of the AI boom. Then again, perhaps thinking about it now is simply a habit of right-side thinking. (

As for why this occurred to me, it was probably because of mythos. I saw what it could do in the security field, and I think that struck a nerve, making me realize that they were never content to remain within coding alone.

That realization then reminded me of their work and promotion in video, as well as their experiments in music composition. In truth, they stopped being satisfied with the field of code long ago. Once an industry moves from its expansion phase into a plateau, will the cost of using its products remain as low as it is today?

As someone without much capital, all I can do is let the times push me forward, feeling my way ahead without knowing whether I am right or wrong. Perhaps that really is tragic. Yet the opportunities of this new world are also laid out equally before everyone. Perhaps that, too, is a gift from the times.
