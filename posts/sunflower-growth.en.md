How are those of us who are still children supposed to grow into adults?

Come to think of it, this really is the kind of question only bored children would ask.

And yet, it is precisely their longing for the future and their devotion to what they hold dear (meaning children, right? (just kidding)) that guide them toward adulthood.

As for the older generation, Daigo, Akira, and Momiji were all held back by Akari, who never grew up, and left behind in the astronomy club of their youth. Given Akari’s fragility, that wish would indeed be better described as a curse. Of course, her very existence also set those three on the paths they now follow, allowed Soichiro’s research to continue, and quite literally gave humanity an enemy that devours human souls. From a post-2050 perspective—and as an outsider—I should unequivocally call it a mistake. But for them, life truly comes only once. Perhaps when you race headlong down your own path, only in hindsight can you say with certainty that what you did back then was wrong. While all of this can indeed be attributed, in a sense, to Akari’s fragility, it must also be said that thanks to her, Yoichi was able to have his beloved Aoi, Aqua, Aries, and Asuka to walk beside him, as well as his adorable little sister Cosmos (so where is Cosmos-chan’s route? (cries) Also, Momiji really does spend the entire story getting hurt).

Back in 2050, Yoichi is still trapped in the maze of the past. Even with the club president and Asuka beside him, he remains sheltered within a fragile lie. His encounter with Aries is precisely what changes everything. The past stirring beneath the surface cannot be escaped if he wishes to move forward. Whether through his mutual salvation with Aqua or Aries’s game of impersonation (please give us more of Aoi’s story (cries)), Aoi has never once vanished from his heart. Thinking about it this way, Asuka really is incredible: with both her maturity and her vulnerability, she attracts Yoichi’s attention, guides him toward the future, and chooses to walk into that future together with him—not to mention the ultimate HE after Aqua After. Now that is the status of the true heroine (so could you please spare just a little of the story for Cosmos? Please, I’m begging you, cry).

The other underlying plot thread should be the virus from outer space. I never expected Another Side to introduce yet another Alice Group and the Vessel of God. Goo, you really do love digging holes for yourself. Returning to the main storyline, the timeline makes it fairly clear that the virus appeared alongside the silver sphere around 1995 or 1996. Then, at some unknown point, the Alice Group created a cloned infant girl who was implanted with a silver sphere and developed Alice Syndrome, while Soichiro stole some of the virus’s DNA. Then came 2029, when Daigo landed on the Moon and brought back a fragment of silver metal carrying RNA, officially beginning the research into inherited memories. Next came the virus leak in 2033, which led Soichiro to discover the hope that Akari’s DNA offered for memory inheritance, pushing research into the Luna Theory into the human-experimentation stage. Then, sometime before August 23, Daigo and Akari had breakup sex (of course you two cheated during a pregnancy; neither of you is any good 😡), causing Daigo to become infected with the Luna Virus and resulting in Cosmos-chan’s birth. The timeline then advances to 2048. The Luna experiments have essentially stalled, and Soichiro, crushed by guilt, decides to destroy the Luna Virus. Prompted by an accidental remark from Cosmos, Daigo heads to Himawari (and suffers an outbreak along the way), bringing light to Aqua before dying. Soichiro also discovers that Aoi has fallen ill. On his return journey, he even stages a little locked-room mystery on an uninhabited island (not really), obtains a list of every infected person, and arranges the doomed flight. It is also around this time that Aoi (?) infects Yoichi with the virus and chooses to leave. Finally, the date reaches August 23, the day fate begins to turn: Daigo dies, Momiji falls ill, everyone aboard SA-DAN080 perishes, and Yoichi suffers shock-induced amnesia after discovering Aoi’s body. Yoichi then meets Asuka and begins his post-amnesia life. At last, on March 20, 2050, Yoichi meets Aries, and the wheels of fate begin turning once again.

Then there is Aqua After. Aside from the married couple’s quarrel, the most important thing is probably that Cosmos-chan finally gets to appear. Even if she does sneak a bite (just kidding), I still wish she had a proper route that could actually be pursued. Her appearance also lifts part of the veil surrounding exactly what memories the virus carries and what effects a virus capable of crossing the blood-brain barrier might have on humanity. Whether humanity and the virus can ultimately coexist may still depend on this tiny girl, ww (so can we please pursue Cosmos-chan? /(ㄒoㄒ)/\~\~)
