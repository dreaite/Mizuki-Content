## **1．Objectives**

- Understand the basic structure of an email system
- Understand communication between clients and servers, as well as between servers
- Analyze and understand the SMTP and POP3 protocols

## **2．Experimental Environment**

- Hardware requirement: One Alibaba Cloud ECS instance.
- Software requirement: Linux/Windows operating system

## **3．Experimental Tasks**


### **3.1 Sending and Receiving Email Using a Mail User Agent**


Configure a mail user agent (such as `windows` `Outlook` `Express`, or Thunderbird on `linux`; you may use the software included with the operating system or download one yourself) to compose emails locally and receive emails from your QQ Mail account.


### **3.2 Sending and Receiving Email Locally**


Use a local client to compose and send QQ emails. At the same time, use `Wireshark` to capture packets and analyze the communication process and protocols.


### **3.3 Webmail**


Log in to QQ Mail using a browser, then compose and send QQ emails. At the same time, use `Wireshark` to capture packets and analyze the communication process and protocols.


### **3.4 Sending and Receiving Email via Telnet**


Use the `telnet` command to log in to the QQ Mail server and send emails. At the same time, use `Wireshark` to capture packets and analyze the communication process and protocols.


Look up the specific commands and ports online.
